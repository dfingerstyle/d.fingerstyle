# d.fingerstyle
Daniel Helmel | Guitarist · Composer · Arranger · Cover Artist

I'm Daniel Helmel, a 14-year-old guitarist known online as D.Fingerstyle.

I've been playing guitar for approximately 1.2 years. My musical journey started with classical guitar, eventually leading me through electric guitar and flamenco before discovering modern percussive fingerstyle.

I genuinely love playing guitar. My goal isn't simply to become technically impressive. I want to create music that makes people feel something: beautiful melodies, emotional arrangements, memorable compositions and challenging guitar pieces that push the boundaries of what's possible on an acoustic guitar.

I'm currently primarily focused on composing arrangements and covers of existing songs, with the ambition of writing more completely original music in the future.
