# Musical Influences

My listening and playing interests cross acoustic fingerstyle, rock, metal, classical music, blues and flamenco. I am drawn to music that brings together beautiful melodies, emotional expression and technical challenge.

## Fingerstyle & Acoustic Guitar

- Alex Misko
- Andy McKee
- Eric Roche
- Mike Dawes
- Don Ross
- Justin King

Discovering Alex Misko introduced me to modern percussive fingerstyle and helped point me toward combining melody, rhythm, harmonics, tapping, slapping and acoustic percussion. My first fingerstyle arrangement was heavily inspired by Alex Misko's 2014 arrangement, while I independently wrote most of my arrangement.

The artists above are part of the acoustic and fingerstyle music I enjoy. I am still developing my own approach and learning from a wide range of music.

## Rock & Alternative Music

- Nirvana
- Metallica

I also enjoy rock and alternative music, alongside metal and progressive music. Exploring different genres gives me musical ideas beyond acoustic guitar alone.

## Other Musical Interests

- Classical guitar and classical music
- Blues
- Flamenco
- Metal and progressive music

I enjoy discovering music regardless of style, especially when it offers a strong melody, expressive character or an interesting musical idea to adapt for guitar.
