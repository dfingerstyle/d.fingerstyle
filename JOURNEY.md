# My Guitar Journey

I'm Daniel Helmel, a guitarist and musician known online as D.Fingerstyle. I began playing approximately 1.2 years ago. My path has moved from classical guitar through electric guitar and flamenco to modern percussive fingerstyle. I am still learning and finding my musical identity.

## Classical Guitar: The Beginning

I started on an inexpensive blue classical guitar. I began taking lessons, but eventually stopped attending regularly. Some time later, I felt a renewed motivation to play and started taking guitar much more seriously.

## Electric Guitar: A New Side

After several months of playing, I got a black Vision Stratocaster. It was originally set up for a left-handed player, and I converted it to right-handed configuration.

I later acquired a 2006 ebony Epiphone Les Paul Standard. I modified its neck humbucker with a Greeny-inspired modification to explore the out-of-phase sound associated with Peter Green. This period introduced me to electric guitar and a broader range of rock sounds.

## Flamenco: Discovering Percussion

In January, I began learning flamenco guitar. It was my first real introduction to percussive playing, with rhythmic strikes and percussion becoming part of the guitar performance.

## Modern Percussive Fingerstyle: Finding My Direction

In February, I acquired my first acoustic guitar, a Bromo BAA4CE. After discovering Alex Misko, I became fascinated with modern percussive fingerstyle: bringing melody, rhythm, harmonics, tapping, slapping and acoustic percussion together on one guitar.

My first fingerstyle arrangement was heavily inspired by Alex Misko's 2014 arrangement, though I independently wrote most of my own arrangement. That was the beginning of my journey into modern acoustic fingerstyle.

## Where I Am Now

I am primarily focused on composing arrangements and covers of existing songs. I want to keep improving my percussive fingerstyle technique, exploring alternate tunings and developing original compositions over time.

My goal is not only to become technically impressive. I want to make music that makes people feel something: beautiful melodies, emotional arrangements, memorable compositions and challenging pieces that explore what an acoustic guitar can do.
