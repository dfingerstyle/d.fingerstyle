# D.Fingerstyle

**Daniel Helmel | Guitarist · Composer · Arranger · Cover Artist**

I'm Daniel Helmel, a 14-year-old guitarist known online as **D.Fingerstyle**.

I've been playing guitar for approximately 1.2 years. My musical journey started with classical guitar, eventually leading me through electric guitar and flamenco before discovering modern percussive fingerstyle.

I genuinely love playing guitar. My goal isn't simply to become technically impressive. I want to create music that makes people feel something: beautiful melodies, emotional arrangements, memorable compositions and challenging guitar pieces that push the boundaries of what's possible on an acoustic guitar.

I'm currently primarily focused on composing arrangements and covers of existing songs, with the ambition of writing more completely original music in the future.

---

## 🎸 My Musical Journey

### Classical Guitar — The Beginning

I started playing on an inexpensive blue classical guitar. After beginning lessons, I eventually stopped attending regularly. Some time later, I experienced a renewed motivation to play and began taking guitar much more seriously.

### Electric Guitar — Discovering a New Side

After several months of playing, I acquired a black Vision Stratocaster, originally left-handed, which I converted to right-handed playing.

I subsequently acquired a 2006 ebony Epiphone Les Paul Standard. I modified its neck humbucker with a Greeny-inspired modification, exploring the characteristic out-of-phase sound associated with Peter Green.

This period introduced me to electric guitar playing and a wider range of rock sounds.

### Flamenco — Discovering Percussion

In January, I began learning flamenco guitar. This was my first real introduction to percussive guitar playing, incorporating rhythmic strikes and percussion into my playing.

### Modern Percussive Fingerstyle — Finding My Direction

In February, I acquired my first acoustic guitar, a Bromo BAA4CE.

After discovering Alex Misko, I became fascinated with modern percussive fingerstyle, combining melody, rhythm, harmonics, tapping, slapping and acoustic percussion.

My first fingerstyle arrangement was heavily inspired by Alex Misko's 2014 arrangement, although I independently wrote most of the arrangement myself.

This was the beginning of my journey into modern acoustic fingerstyle.
