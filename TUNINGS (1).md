## Acoustic Strings

- I started with Elixir 12–56 strings.
- I later moved toward D'Addario acoustic strings, especially Phosphor Bronze and 80/20 Bronze.
- I use 13–56 gauges for lower tunings and prefer heavier strings for down-tuned acoustic playing.
- I use them on all my guitars since i started using daddario i had way way way lesss string breakage especially on my g string which i had alot of problems with
- also experimenting with rc13 Sunbeams roundcore phosphor bronze strings for jon gomm tone
