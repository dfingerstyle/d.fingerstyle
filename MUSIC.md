# Music & Projects

My current creative focus is arranging and covering existing songs for guitar. I am especially interested in adapting music to acoustic guitar and finding ways to recreate musical ideas with melody, rhythm, harmonics, tapping, slapping and acoustic percussion.

## Arrangements & Covers

I am composing arrangements and covers, with a particular interest in expressive and technically challenging fingerstyle pieces. My first fingerstyle arrangement was heavily inspired by Alex Misko's 2014 arrangement, while I independently wrote most of my arrangement.

I have not listed individual song titles or release links here yet.

## Original Compositions

I have not yet established myself as an original songwriter, but developing my own compositions and musical identity is an important goal. I want to write music with memorable melodies and emotional impact, while continuing to explore the possibilities of acoustic guitar.

## Sharing & Future Plans

- Share more covers and original music online.
- Develop original compositions.
- Eventually publish guitar tabs and arrangements.
- Continue creating arrangements that balance musicality with technical challenge.

I am still learning, experimenting and developing as a musician. I play because I genuinely love playing guitar, and I want to keep discovering what I can create with it.
